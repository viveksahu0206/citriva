Frequently Answered Questions

How does Citriva prevent hallucination in verdicts?

Temperature is pinned to 0; models judge exclusively from supplied passages without using parametric world knowledge; and every returned evidence quote must be a verified character-for-character substring in code.

What export formats are supported?

Citriva compiles complete, publication-grade academic PDF reports (strictly PDF only) formatted for peer-review panels, journal editors, and grant audit boards.
