Citriva is an AI-powered scientific claim and citation verification platform that helps researchers and students check whether the sources they cite actually support the claims they make. Instead of simply checking whether a research paper exists, Citriva analyzes the claim against the cited paper, identifies supporting or conflicting evidence, and highlights unsupported claims, research gaps, and overclaims. Users can upload an academic PDF, and Citriva automatically extracts claims and references, retrieves relevant scientific literature, analyzes the evidence, and provides clear verification results such as **Supported, Partially Supported, or Unsupported**. By connecting every result to the actual evidence from the source paper, Citriva makes academic research faster, more transparent, trustworthy, and easier to verify.
